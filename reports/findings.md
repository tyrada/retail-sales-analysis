# Findings from the deterministic 10,000-row sample

- Processed **10,000** input rows: **9,428** accepted, **172** invalid, and **400** duplicates.
- Accepted revenue was **$509,977.30** across 24 months.
- The highest-revenue month was **2024-06** at **$23,978.97**.
- The highest-revenue product was **Laptop Stand** at **$57,847.60**.
- **999** customers placed at least two distinct orders.
- The most frequent product pair was **Canvas Tote + Desk Lamp**, appearing in **37** orders.

Co-purchase associations describe items observed in the same order. They do not establish that purchasing one item causes purchasing another; promotions, seasonality, customer preferences, and the synthetic generation process can all confound the relationship.
